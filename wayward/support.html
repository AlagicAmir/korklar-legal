Frequently asked questions

Why did my point land somewhere boring?

Wayward only promises a point that is public and reachable on foot — a park, a path, a street or a viewpoint — not that it will be interesting. Every point is genuinely random within your chosen radius. If a spot is a letdown, tap "Redraw" (uses one quest) or try a different radius; the mission is often more rewarding than the location itself.

Why does it say "nothing reachable"?

Wayward checks up to 40 candidate points, widening the search radius up to three times, before it gives up. If your area has very little public, walkable land within range — for example you are surrounded by water, farmland or a motorway — the app tells you honestly rather than sending you somewhere private or unreachable. Try a larger radius, or draw again from a different spot.

How do the three free quests a day work?

Every account gets three quest draws per calendar day at no cost, with radii up to 3 km. The count resets at midnight, local time. Redrawing a quest uses one of your three. Wayward Pro removes the daily limit and unlocks the 10 km radius, the full mission library and an unlimited quest log.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone and cancel there. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

I bought Pro and it is not active on my new phone

Open Settings › Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my data?

Your quest log, photos and notes exist only on your device. Delete individual quests in the app, or delete the app itself to remove everything at once. To have purchase data held by our processor (RevenueCat) deleted, email us at the address above.

Is Wayward affiliated with Randonautica or any similar app?

No. Wayward is an independent app built from scratch and has no affiliation with Randonautica or any other "random location" app.
