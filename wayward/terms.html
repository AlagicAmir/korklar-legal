2. What the App Does

Wayward uses your location, Apple MapKit and public OpenStreetMap land-use data to draw a random point that is public and reachable on foot — a park, a path, a street or a viewpoint, never water, a motorway or private land — guides you there with a compass, and gives you a mission drawn from a seed bank when you arrive. You can keep a quest log with photos and notes on your device, and generate a shareable summary card.
