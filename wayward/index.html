Wayward: Random Adventure

A random, reachable place near you, and a mission when you get there · Amir Alagic (enskild firma), Sweden.

Wayward draws a random public point near you — a park, a path, a street or a viewpoint, never water, a motorway or private land — guides you there with a compass, and gives you a mission when you arrive. Your quest log, photos and notes stay on your device. There is no Wayward server.
