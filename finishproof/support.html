FinishProof Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to support@korlogg.com. Please include your phone model, Android version and the app version shown under Settings.

Where is my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account and no cloud, so we cannot see or restore your jobs. Use Settings › Export backup regularly and keep the file somewhere safe.

Moving to a new phone

Export a backup on the old phone, copy the ZIP file to the new phone and choose Settings › Restore backup. Existing jobs are kept.

Deleting your data

Delete a job from its menu, or remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Location stamp

Turn it on under Settings and allow location access. Coordinates are added to photos you take from then on and shown on the photo in reports.
