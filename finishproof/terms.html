What the app does

FinishProof helps you record work and create PDF reports. A report records what the user entered. It does not certify an independent inspection or customer acceptance, and amounts shown are a work record, not an invoice.

Your responsibility

Check job details, photos and checklist status before you share a report. Use the app only for work and material you are authorised to document, and follow the rules that apply to taking photos and handling customer information.

Your data and backups

Your data is stored only on your device. We have no copy and cannot recover it. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes it. Export backups regularly, keep them in a safe place and check that they can be restored.
