In short

FinishProof collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no server of ours. Everything you record stays on your device until you share it yourself. The only exception is a FinishProof Pro subscription: the purchase goes through Google Play and RevenueCat, as described under Purchases.

What is stored on your device

Job names, customer details, addresses, notes, checklists, time and material lines, your business name and logo, and resized copies of your photos. When a customer signs, the app also stores the name, the signature and the time. Voice notes you record are kept as audio files on your device. A plan you add is kept as a picture.

Location

The app asks for location access only if you turn on the location stamp. The coordinates are then saved with each photo you take and appear in your reports. If a photo you import already contains a location, that location is kept with the copy; all other photo metadata is removed. Location is never sent to us.

Camera, microphone and files

The app uses the camera only while you take a photo and the microphone only while you record a voice note. It opens the system file picker when you import a photo and does not read your gallery on its own.

Sharing and backups

You can share a PDF report or export a ZIP backup using Android. Copies you save or share are handled by the destination you choose and stay there until you or the recipient delete them. ZIP backups are not encrypted by FinishProof.

Keeping and deleting

Data stays in the app until you delete it, clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Archiving a job keeps its data. We have no copy and cannot recover deleted data.

Your customers’ data

If you record information about customers or other people, you are responsible for it. Follow the rules that apply to your business for photographing people or property and for sharing reports. If you have questions about the information in a report, contact the business that gave it to you.

Purchases

If you subscribe to FinishProof Pro, the purchase is handled by Google Play, which processes your payment under its own privacy policy. The app only receives confirmation that the subscription is active. We do not see your card or payment details. Nothing you record in the app is sent along. To confirm the subscription, the app uses the service RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA). RevenueCat receives a random identifier for your installation of the app, the purchase record from Google Play and technical details such as the app version, the Android version and your country. It does not receive your name or email address.
