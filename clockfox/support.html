Frequently asked questions

I was not clocked in automatically

iPhone: check that location access for ClockFox is set to Always with Precise Location on, that notifications are allowed, and that Clock in and out automatically is on in Settings › Tracking. Settings › Tracking › Tracking check shows what is missing, the last 20 arrivals and departures and a log of what the app decided. Android: set location to Allow all the time with precise location, allow notifications, set battery use for ClockFox to Unrestricted (some phones otherwise stop apps in the background), and check that Automatic clocking is on in the app's settings; Settings › Automatic clocking › Tracking check shows what is missing. ClockFox never clocks you in at the home you saved, and a job set to Ask before clocking in sends a notification instead. You can always clock in by hand, or add a shift afterwards.

The times are a few minutes off

ClockFox waits a few minutes before it counts an arrival or a departure, so walking past the door does not start a shift; the shift itself starts at the time you arrived. On iPhone you can change both waiting times in Settings › Tracking; on Android you can change the arrival time in Settings › Automatic clocking (leaving is counted after 5 minutes away). A larger workplace radius helps if the building is big or the signal is weak. You can edit any shift, and the app keeps the original times in the shift's change log.

I forgot to clock out

If you are still clocked in after leaving, ClockFox asks "Still at work?" and offers to clock you out at the time you left. You can also open the shift and set the end time.

Does the app drain the battery?

No. Automatic clocking uses the phone's own low-power place monitoring (region monitoring on iPhone, geofences on Android), not constant GPS. Location is used more only for a few minutes around an arrival or a departure (and, on Android, as a low-power position about every ten minutes while "Is this your work?" suggestions are on).

Can my employer see where I am?

No. There is no account and no ClockFox server. Your employer only sees a report you choose to export and send.

Are the overtime and holiday rules correct for my country?

The country you choose sets defaults for the week, overtime, breaks and public holidays. They are a starting point only: check them against your contract or collective agreement, and change them under Settings › Work rules or for each job.

How do I keep a backup or move to a new phone?

ClockFox makes a backup file every day (the newest 30 are kept). It is a sealed .clockfoxbackup file that only ClockFox, on iPhone or Android, can restore. On iPhone it is in iCloud Drive › ClockFox › Backups, or in Files › On My iPhone › ClockFox › Backups without iCloud Drive. On Android it is kept in the app's storage and included in Android's own backup when that is on for your Google account. You can also tap Settings › Data › Back up now and save the file wherever you like. On the new phone, use Restore from file; the app shows what the file contains before it replaces anything. Backup and restore are always included, also without Pro. A readable JSON copy of your data is a Pro export.

What is included in Pro?

Without Pro you get automatic clocking for up to three jobs with no limit on shifts, full details and editing for the last 30 days, weekly and monthly hours for your whole history, holidays, day notes, the status widget, and backup and restore. Pro adds all reports and exports (PDF, Excel, CSV, JSON, payroll) for any period, the full history, earnings with overtime and premiums, flexitime balance and leave, more than three jobs, pay periods, planned shifts, the year view and all widgets. Older shifts are never deleted automatically; they are waiting for you when you upgrade.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from. On iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge; you keep Pro until the end of the paid period. There is no free trial, and the lifetime purchase is a one-time payment that never renews.

I bought Pro and it is not active on my new phone

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases in the Pro section at the top, while signed in with the same Apple Account (iPhone) or Google account (Android). A purchase belongs to the store it was made in: Pro bought on iPhone does not carry over to Android, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. On iPhone go to reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android use Google Play's order history. Refunds follow their policies.

Which languages are supported?

English, Swedish, German, Spanish and Polish. You can choose the language under Settings › Appearance and format.

Privacy and deletion

See the Privacy Policy. Your hours are on your phone: delete single shifts in the app, or everything with Settings › Data › Delete all data, which also removes your home, export details and the backups saved on the phone. Backups in iCloud Drive are kept unless you choose Also delete backups in iCloud Drive. Deleting the app removes everything on the phone. To delete purchase data held by our processor, e-mail us with the subject "ClockFox data deletion".
