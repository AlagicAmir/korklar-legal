Common questions

How does the units calculator work?

It divides the peptide amount in the vial by the water you added to get a concentration, then converts the dose you enter into units on a U-100 insulin syringe (100 units = 1 ml). It is arithmetic on your own numbers; it never suggests a dose. Check the syringe you actually have.

Why does the app not tell me when to increase my dose?

Because that is your prescriber's decision. The plan screen shows the manufacturer's published schedule for reference and nothing more.

How do I cancel Jabwise Plus?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Jabwise. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active.

How do I delete my data?

More → Delete all my data, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
