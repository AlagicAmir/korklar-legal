Privacy Policy for Kjøreklar

Last Updated: 3 September 2026 · Personvernerklæring – norsk sammendrag nederst.

Our Privacy Commitment

We don't like apps that harvest and sell user data. Kjøreklar does not do this.

Kjøreklar is a Norwegian driving-theory practice app for the klasse B (car) theory test (teoriprøven), developed by an independent sole proprietor based in Sweden. We never sell your data, we don't run advertising networks, and we keep your personal data on your own device.

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app uses and how your privacy is protected.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Kjøreklar is local-first: the information below is stored only on your device (using Apple's UserDefaults and SwiftData) and is not transmitted to us or to any server we control.

Personalisation:

  • An optional first name you may enter to personalise the app (shown on the home screen). You can leave it blank. It stays on your device.

Learning progress & statistics:

  • Which questions and categories you've answered, and whether correctly
  • Quiz and mock-exam results, scores and pass/fail history
  • Study streaks, time spent and activity dates

Settings & preferences:

  • Selected interface language (Norwegian Bokmål or English)
  • Notification preference (daily reminder on/off)
  • Premium entitlement status

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases (In-App Purchase)

Kjøreklar offers an optional one-time Premium unlock through Apple's In-App Purchase system. To manage purchases and restore them across your devices, we use RevenueCat, a purchase-management service acting as our data processor.

  • The actual payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user ID, your purchase/receipt information and basic device/app data in order to validate the purchase and unlock Premium. This identifier is not your name and is not used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • We use this information only to grant and restore your Premium access.

RevenueCat's own privacy information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts, no email, no passwords, no third-party login. You simply enter a first name to start; it is stored only on your device.
  • No advertising or ad identifiers (IDFA). We display no ads.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or profiling SDKs.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts access.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data — ever.

How We Use Information

The on-device data is used solely to track your progress and resume where you left off, calculate statistics (streaks, accuracy, exam history), save your preferences, and grant the Premium features you have purchased.

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share personal data for profit. The only third parties involved are Apple (app distribution and payment processing, subject to Apple's privacy policy) and RevenueCat (purchase validation and restoration, as described above). No data brokers, no advertising networks, no social-media trackers.

Your Rights (GDPR)

As a Sweden/EU-based developer we follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which also applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement and the Norwegian Personal Data Act (personopplysningsloven). Because your personal data lives on your device, you are in direct control of it:

  • Access & portability: view your progress and statistics inside the app
  • Rectification: change your name and preferences at any time
  • Erasure: delete all local data by deleting the app; manage purchases via your Apple ID / App Store account — see Request data deletion
  • Objection/restriction: you can use the app without entering a name and without making any purchase

For questions about purchase data held by our processor, or to exercise any GDPR right, contact us (below). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: the Norwegian Datatilsynet or the Swedish Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Children's Privacy

Kjøreklar does not knowingly collect personal data from children. As all data stays on the device and no profiles are created, the app is safe for users of all ages. Driving-theory study is generally aimed at users preparing for a licence (from 15–16 years in Norway).

Data Security

  • iOS App Sandbox isolation and on-device encryption protect stored data
  • No transmission of your personal data to servers we operate
  • Purchase data is transmitted securely between Apple, RevenueCat and your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app changes or legal requirements. We will update the "Last Updated" date and, for significant changes, provide notice within the app.
