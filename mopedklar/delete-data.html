Mopedklar — Begära radering av data / Request data deletion

Last updated: 30 August 2026

App: Mopedklar — Moped & A-traktor (AM-körkort). Developer: Amir Alagic, enskild firma, Sweden (EU).

1. Data stored on your device — delete the app

Mopedklar is local-first. Your name, answers, quiz and mock-exam results, streaks, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it immediately. No request to us is needed and nothing is left behind on our side, because we never had it.

2. Purchase data — email us

The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to validate a purchase. To request its deletion, email avelan.sverige@gmail.com with the subject “Mopedklar — data deletion request”. Tell us which store you purchased from (Google Play or App Store) and the approximate purchase date. We reply within 1–2 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

You do not need an account — Mopedklar has none.

We do not run advertising networks or analytics SDKs, so there is no profile, tracking history or ad identifier to delete.

4. Your rights

As an EU (Sweden) developer we follow the GDPR. You may request access, rectification or erasure at the address above, and you may lodge a complaint with Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).
