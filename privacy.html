Privacy Policy for Körklar

Last Updated: June 14, 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

We don't like apps that harvest and sell user data. Körklar does not do this.

Körklar is a Swedish driving-theory (B-körkort) practice app developed by an

independent sole proprietor based in Sweden. **We never sell your data, we don't

run advertising networks, and we keep your personal data on your own device.**

This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app uses

and how your privacy is protected.

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Körklar is local-first: the information below is stored only on your device

(using Apple's UserDefaults and SwiftData) and is **not transmitted to us or to

any server we control**.

Personalisation:

  • An optional first name you may enter to personalise the app (e.g. shown on

the home screen). You can leave it blank. It stays on your device.

Learning progress & statistics:

  • Which questions and categories you've answered, and whether correctly
  • Quiz and mock-exam results, scores and pass/fail history
  • Bookmarked questions and questions you got wrong
  • Study streaks, time spent and activity dates

Settings & preferences:

  • Selected interface language (Swedish, English, German, French, Spanish,

Turkish, Arabic or Persian)

  • Notification preference (daily reminder on/off)
  • Premium entitlement status

You can erase all of this at any time by deleting the app.

Purchases (In-App Purchase)

Körklar offers an optional one-time Premium unlock through Apple's In-App

Purchase system. To manage purchases and restore them across your devices, we use

RevenueCat, a purchase-management service acting as our data processor.

  • The actual payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or receive

your card number, bank details or billing address.

  • RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user ID, your purchase/receipt

information and basic device/app data in order to validate the purchase and

unlock Premium. This identifier is not your name and is not used to track you

across other apps or websites.

  • We use this information only to grant and restore your Premium access.

RevenueCat's own privacy information: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts, no email, no passwords, no third-party login. You simply enter

a first name to start; it is stored only on your device and nothing is sent to

or stored on any server we operate.

  • No advertising or ad identifiers (IDFA). We display no ads.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or profiling SDKs.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts access.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data — ever.

How We Use Information

The on-device data is used solely to:

  • Track your progress and resume where you left off
  • Calculate statistics (streaks, accuracy, exam history)
  • Save your preferences (language, theme, reminders)
  • Grant the Premium features you have purchased

Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share personal data for profit. The only third parties involved are:

  • Apple — app distribution and payment processing (subject to Apple's privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and restoration, as described above

No data brokers, no advertising networks, no social-media trackers.

Your Rights (GDPR)

As a Sweden/EU-based developer we follow the **General Data Protection

Regulation (GDPR)**. Because your personal data lives on your device, you are in

direct control of it:

  • Access & portability: view your progress and statistics inside the app
  • Rectification: change your name and preferences at any time
  • Erasure: delete all local data by deleting the app; manage purchases via

your Apple ID / App Store account

  • Objection/restriction: you can use the app without entering a name and

without making any purchase

For questions about purchase data held by our processor, or to exercise any GDPR

right, contact us (below). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the

Swedish data protection authority, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

Children's Privacy

Körklar does not knowingly collect personal data from children. As all data

stays on the device and no profiles are created, the app is safe for users of all

ages. Driving-theory study is generally aimed at users preparing for a licence.

Data Security

  • iOS App Sandbox isolation and on-device encryption protect stored data
  • No transmission of your personal data to servers we operate
  • Purchase data is transmitted securely between Apple, RevenueCat and your device
  • Security is maintained through regular App Store updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app changes or legal requirements.

We will update the "Last Updated" date and, for significant changes, provide

notice within the app.
