Privacy Policy for E-Rechnung (Android)

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Our promise

Invoices contain names, addresses, amounts and bank details. That is why your invoices, customers and receipts stay on your device. E-Rechnung is an app for writing and checking German e-invoices (ZUGFeRD and XRechnung), made by a sole trader in Sweden. There is no account and no server of ours. We show no ads, the app contains no analytics or tracking tools, and your invoices, customers and receipts never reach us. Only to check your Pro purchase does the app use the RevenueCat service (see “Purchases”); RevenueCat receives no invoice data.

Data stored on your device

Only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your business: name, address, contact details, tax number (Steuernummer), VAT ID (USt-IdNr.), bank details, your VAT choice and the number series.
  • Invoices, quotes, credit notes and reminders with customers, line items, amounts, dates and payment status.
  • Customers you add under “More” or that are saved when you lock an invoice, and your items and services with prices.
  • Expenses you enter yourself or take over from received e-invoices.

If you have turned on your Android device's backup (Google backup, or the move to a new device), the data you have entered is part of it. Android makes this backup, not we, and we have no access to it. If you delete the app, the data on the device is deleted. So keep invoices you must retain as files as well (see the terms of use).

Received files

When you open an e-invoice (PDF or XML) from an email, from your files or from another app, E-Rechnung reads it on the device and does not keep it. A received invoice is stored only when you take it over as an expense.

CSV import: when you import hours (for example from ClockFox) or business trips (from Körlogg) as a CSV file, the app reads the file on the device and turns it into invoice line items. The file itself is not saved and not passed on.

When data leaves your device

  • When you share: PDF, XML, the DATEV file and the adviser package (a ZIP with your invoices and expenses for one year) are created on the device and handed to the share sheet. You decide where to send them; the recipient's terms apply.
  • Purchases: Pro is bought through Google Play (in-app purchase). Google handles the payment; we never see your card number or bank details. At Google Play we see orders with their order number, product, amount and country, and nothing from the app: no invoices, customers or amounts. Google Play's billing sends its own technical details to Google while doing so (device model, Android version, language, country and network type). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Checking and restoring Pro: for this the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), a service that processes this data on our behalf. The app contacts RevenueCat when it starts and when you view, buy or restore Pro. RevenueCat receives:
    • an anonymous ID that the app generates (not your name and not an advertising ID),
    • the purchase receipt and transaction data from Google Play (which product, when it was bought, renewed, cancelled or refunded),
    • the device type, Android version, app version and your Play Store country,
    • and, as with any internet connection, your IP address.
    No invoices, customers, amounts, bank details, names or email addresses go to RevenueCat. We use this data only to unlock and restore Pro for you. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Support: “Email support” opens your own mail app with our address, the app version, the Android version and the device type. You can change or delete anything before sending. We use emails only to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed.
  • Ratings: “Rate the app” opens the app's page in Google Play, and the request for a rating is shown by Google Play; what you do there goes to Google, not to us.
  • App lock: if you turn it on, Android asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app only learns whether the device was unlocked; your biometric data stays with Android.
  • Links to these pages open in your browser.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours. The app makes no network connections of its own except to Google and RevenueCat for purchases.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, no advertising identifier. RevenueCat is not an analytics tool, but it is a third party that receives the purchase data described above.
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

The app processes the data in it for you, on your device; we do not receive it. We process the purchase data at RevenueCat to perform the contract when you buy Pro (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. If you email us, we process your email on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. To use these rights, write to the address below. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Sweden the Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), in Germany the data protection authority of your federal state.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we update this page and the date above.
