1. Agreement

E-Rechnung (the “App”) is made by a sole trader in Sweden. By using the App you agree to these terms.

2. What the App does

With the App you write invoices, quotes, credit notes and reminders as German e-invoices (ZUGFeRD PDF and XRechnung XML according to EN 16931), lock them and send them. You open and check received e-invoices, pay them by GiroCode and take them over as expenses. Before an invoice is locked, the App checks the required details under EN 16931 and § 14 UStG (German VAT Act).

Language: the App's screens can be English, but the documents it creates (invoices, quotes, reminders, their XML, the DATEV file and the adviser package) are always in German, as German customers and tax offices expect.

3. No tax or legal advice

The App is a tool. It does not replace tax or legal advice. The check finds common mistakes but cannot guarantee that an invoice is correct for tax purposes in every case (for example the right VAT rate or the right VAT treatment). You are responsible for the content of your invoices. If in doubt, ask your tax adviser (Steuerberater).

4. Keeping your invoices

In Germany, invoices usually have to be kept for eight to ten years. The App stores your data only on your device. If you delete the App or lose the device, the data is gone unless you have a backup. Back up your device and also keep the PDF and XML files you sent, for example in your mailbox, in Files or with your tax adviser.

5. Free and Pro

  • Free: open and check received e-invoices without limit, and lock three invoices. Quotes, credit notes (cancellations) and reminders are not limited.
  • Pro removes the limit for invoices. Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. You see the prices in the App before buying.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play under “Payments and subscriptions”. Refunds are handled by Google under Google Play's rules.
  • A purchase applies to your Google Account and can be unlocked on a new Android device with “Restore purchases”. Purchases in Google Play and in Apple's App Store are separate.
