Privacy Policy for E-Rechnung

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Our promise

Invoices contain names, addresses, amounts and bank details. That is why your invoices, customers and receipts stay on your device. E-Rechnung is an app for writing and checking German e-invoices (ZUGFeRD and XRechnung), made by a sole trader in Sweden. There is no account and no server of ours. We show no ads, the app contains no analytics or tracking tools, and your data never reaches us.

Data stored on your device

Only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your business: name, address, contact details, tax number (Steuernummer), VAT ID (USt-IdNr.), bank details, your VAT choice and the number series.
  • Invoices, quotes, credit notes and reminders with customers, line items, amounts, dates and payment status.
  • Customers you add under “More” or that are saved when you lock an invoice, and your items and services with prices.
  • Expenses you enter yourself or take over from received e-invoices.

This data is part of your normal device backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have turned it on. If you delete the app, the data is deleted. So keep invoices you must retain as files as well (see the Terms of Use).

Received files

When you open an e-invoice (PDF or XML) from Mail, Files or another app, E-Rechnung reads it on the device. The copy that iOS hands to the app for this is deleted after reading. A received invoice is only saved if you take it over as an expense.

CSV import: when you import hours (for example from ClockFox) or business trips (from Körlogg) as a CSV file, the app reads the file on the device and turns it into invoice line items. The file itself is not saved and not passed on.

When data leaves your device

  • When you share: PDF, XML, the DATEV file and the adviser package (a ZIP with your invoices and expenses for one year) are created on the device and handed to the share sheet. You decide where to send them; the recipient's terms apply.
  • Purchases: Pro is bought through Apple (in-app purchase). The app asks Apple whether a purchase exists. Apple does not give us names or contact details, only aggregated sales figures. Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Support: “Email support” opens your own mail app with our address, the app version, the iOS version and the device type. You can change or delete anything before sending. We use emails only to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed.
  • Ratings: the request for a rating is shown by iOS; what you do there goes to Apple, not to us.
  • Links to these pages open in your browser.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, no advertising identifier.
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

The app processes the data in it for you, on your device; we do not receive it. If you email us, we process your email on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. To use these rights, write to the address below. You can also complain to a supervisory authority: in Sweden the Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), in Germany the data protection authority of your federal state.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we update this page and the date above.
