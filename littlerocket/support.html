Little Rocket Support

Little Rocket is a focus timer where every focus session is a trip through space: your rocket flies while you focus, lands on a new planet at each break, and your galaxy grows with every finished session.

Contact

Questions, ideas or problems? Write to avelan.sverige@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Does the timer keep running if I lock my phone?

Yes. The timer keeps the correct time, and if notifications are allowed you get a notification when focus or the break ends.

Why didn’t I get a new planet?

A planet is discovered only when a focus session is finished. Giving up a session does not count.

How do I start over?

Settings → Reset progress removes your sessions and planets on this device.
