Privacy Policy for Little Rocket

Last Updated: 27 October 2026

In short

Little Rocket collects no personal data that identifies you. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools in the app. The only information that leaves your device is what is needed to process a Pro purchase (see below). Your focus sessions, discovered planets and statistics are stored only on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Your focus and break sessions (start time, length, whether finished), your discovered planets and your settings.
  • This data never leaves your device through the app. Deleting the app, or using “Reset progress” in Settings, removes it.

Purchases (Little Rocket Pro)

Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are paid through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see your name, email address or payment details; Apple and Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies.

To unlock Pro on your device and to restore it later, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that checks purchases on our behalf. RevenueCat receives a random ID created by the app (not linked to your name or email), your purchase receipt and purchase history for Little Rocket, and basic technical details such as the app version, operating system and country of your store account. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

If you would like this purchase data deleted, write to the address below.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Little Rocket schedules local notifications on your device to tell you when a focus session or a break ends. They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

Test versions (TestFlight)

If you test Little Rocket through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share basic information with the developer, such as your name or email address used for the invitation, crash reports, installs and session counts, and any feedback you send. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Little Rocket is a general focus timer for students and adults. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes and contact
