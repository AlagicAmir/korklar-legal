Last Updated: 10 September 2026

Delete all data

Tallwise keeps your profile and logs only on your device. To delete everything: open the app, go to More, and tap Delete all my data. Deleting the app has the same effect.

The only data outside your device is the anonymous purchase record held by RevenueCat for subscription validation. To have it deleted, email avelan.sverige@gmail.com from the device or with your purchase date; we handle it within 30 days.
